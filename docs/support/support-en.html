FCP to AAF Support

FCP to AAF converts FCPXML projects into AAF audio handoffs on Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later.

Getting help

Include the app version, macOS version, Mac chip, receiving application and version, steps to reproduce, export settings, and a redacted log. Do not send confidential client media or credentials.

Supported workflows

The app supports roles, compound clips, explicitly selected multicam audio angles, embedded PCM, external WAV, stereo or mono output, source media handles, volume keyframe positions and values, and supported forward constant-speed processing.

Limitations

Video and OMF export are not supported. Some multicam processing, complex retiming, reverse playback and effects require expansion or rendering in the editing application first.
