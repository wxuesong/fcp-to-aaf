# FCP to AAF Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 20, 2026

## Local processing

FCP to AAF processes projects and audio locally on your Mac. It does not require an account, upload your projects or media, or include advertising, analytics, or tracking services. We do not collect data from the app or sell personal information.

## Files and preferences

The app reads user-selected projects and authorized source media, and writes handoff files to your chosen output location. Preferences and security-scoped bookmarks are stored locally to remember settings and authorized folders.

## Diagnostics

Local logs and diagnostic reports may contain project names, file paths, export settings, and errors. They remain on your Mac unless you choose to share them. You can delete diagnostic files and exported handoffs you no longer need.

## Website and support

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information when you visit it under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Support is provided through GitHub Issues. Reports and attachments are public; submit only information you intend to share. Do not post confidential media, credentials, or unredacted logs. GitHub manages accounts and issue data under its own policies.

## Contact and changes

For privacy questions, [open an issue](https://github.com/wxuesong/fcp-to-aaf/issues) without including sensitive information. We will update this page and its effective date if this policy changes.
