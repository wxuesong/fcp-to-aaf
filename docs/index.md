# Final AAF

Convert Final Cut Pro projects into AAF audio handoffs for dialogue, music, and sound mixing. All processing stays on your Mac.

## Features

- Open FCPXML files and project bundles.
- Expand compound clips and selected multicam audio angles.
- Organize tracks by audio role and preserve volume keyframe positions and values.
- Choose embedded PCM or external WAV, stereo or mono, and full media or trimmed audio with handles.
- Render supported forward constant-speed changes using native macOS audio processing.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 14 or later and access to the original media. Includes English and Simplified Chinese app interfaces. No account or additional runtime installation is needed.
