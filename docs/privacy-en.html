Privacy Policy

FCP to AAF processes projects and media locally on your Mac. It does not require an account and does not upload projects, media, logs, or analytics.

Files are accessed only after you select them or their folders. Diagnostic files remain on your Mac unless you choose to share them. The app does not sell personal information.

For support, contact the developer using the email listed on the App Store product page.
